# Real-life photos for the site

The site shows real photos in the spots below as soon as you save them. No code changes needed. Save each photo in `public/images/lifestyle/` using the exact name (`.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png` or `.webp`), then rebuild or redeploy. A spot without a photo is simply hidden.

| File name | What to photograph | Where it appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `buying-iphone` | A customer holding or pressing an iPhone at the counter | Home (step 3 and the photo mosaic), About |
| `entering-shop` | A customer walking into the shop | Home (step 4 and the mosaic), About |
| `happy-customer` | A smiling customer with a new iPhone or box | Home (step 2 and the mosaic), About |
| `repair-handover` | A customer collecting a repaired phone | Home mosaic, About |
| `delivery` | A delivery being handed to a customer | Home mosaic, About |
| `friends-choosing` | Friends choosing phones together | Home (step 1 and the mosaic), About |

Tips

- Only photograph people who agree to it. Ask first.
- Shoot in daylight, hold the phone steady, and keep the customer's face and the phone in the frame.
- Portrait or landscape both work. Aim for at least 1200 pixels on the long side.
- If you use stock photos, check the licence and do not caption them as your own customers.

Real customer portraits for a "Happy customers" gallery can also be listed in `src/data/reviews.ts` under `customerPhotos`.
